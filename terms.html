The Bot

Description of the Service

4n7 Moderation is a Discord application designed to provide server moderation, administrative, safety, utility, and management tools.

Depending on the configuration of a server, the Service may provide features including:

  • Warnings and warning management
  • Bans, unbans, kicks, and softbans
  • User timeouts through Discord's moderation system
  • Message deletion and channel moderation
  • Slowmode, locking, and unlocking
  • Staff role management
  • Deleted-message and edited-message snipe features
  • Auto moderation and anti-spam protections
  • Moderation records and server logs
  • General server utility commands

Features may change over time. New features may be introduced, and existing features may be modified, disabled, or removed.
