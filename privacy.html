01
Introduction

Privacy Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how 4n7 Moderation ("4n7", "the Bot", "the Service", "we", "us", or "our") processes information when you or a Discord server uses the Service.

This policy applies to information processed through the Discord application and related systems operated for 4n7 Moderation.

!
We aim to collect only what the Bot needs. Information processed by the Bot should be limited to what is reasonably necessary to provide its stated functionality.
02
Information

Data We Process

Depending on how a server uses 4n7 Moderation, the Service may process the following categories of information:

Discord User IDs
Used to identify users involved in commands, warnings, moderation actions, permissions, and snipe records.
Discord Server IDs
Used to associate configuration, moderation information, and other server-specific data with the correct Discord server.
Channel IDs
Used for channel-specific configuration, moderation logs, snipe information, and channel-management functionality.
Role IDs
Used for moderator and administrator permissions, staff-role configuration, and role-management features.
Moderation Records
May include the target user ID, moderator ID, action, reason, duration, and timestamps.
Warning Records
May include the server ID, warned user ID, moderator ID, warning reason, warning identifier, and timestamp.
Message Information
Certain moderation and snipe features may process message IDs, message content, edited content, channel IDs, and author IDs.
Server Configuration
May include configured moderation roles, administrator roles, log channels, AutoMod settings, ignored channels, and other server-specific settings.
03
Collection

How Data Is Collected

Information may be processed automatically when Discord sends relevant event or command information to the Bot.

For example, a moderation command may require a user's Discord ID and the server's Discord ID to identify the target and the server in which the action was requested.

Message-related features may process information when the Bot receives Discord message events needed for AutoMod, deleted-message detection, edited-message detection, or related functionality.

04
Purpose

How We Use Data

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing moderation commands
  • Storing and displaying warnings
  • Recording moderation actions
  • Providing AutoMod functionality
  • Providing deleted-message and edited-message snipe functionality
  • Checking server-specific permissions
  • Applying server-specific configuration
  • Sending configured moderation logs
  • Maintaining, securing, and troubleshooting the Service
i
No unrelated data profiling. API Data should be used only as necessary to provide the Bot's stated functionality and should not be used for unrelated profiling or advertising purposes.

Discord data is processed in accordance with this Privacy Policy, applicable law, and the policies that apply to Discord developers.

06
Disclosure

Data Sharing

4n7 Moderation does not sell Discord API Data. It is not intended to provide Discord data to advertising networks or data brokers.

Data may be provided to service providers that are necessary to operate the Bot, such as hosting or database providers, to the extent necessary to provide the Service.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

Server administrators may also receive moderation information through the Bot's normal functionality, such as moderation logs or warning commands. This is part of the Bot's intended operation.

X
No sale of Discord data. 4n7 Moderation does not sell, license, or otherwise commercially trade Discord API Data.
07
Infrastructure

Storage & Security

Data required by the Service may be stored in databases and other infrastructure used to operate 4n7 Moderation.

The Bot is designed to use identifier-based records so server, channel, role, user, warning, and moderation information can be associated with the correct Discord resources.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

S
Security is an ongoing process. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Security controls may change as the Bot and its infrastructure develop.
08
Retention

Data Retention

Information is intended to be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the feature for which it was collected, satisfy operational requirements, address abuse or security issues, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Warning records may remain available until they are removed by authorized server staff, deleted through an approved data request, or otherwise no longer required.

Moderation logs may be retained to provide server moderation history and security records.

Message information associated with snipe functionality may be retained according to the Bot's configured storage and cleanup mechanisms.

R
Retention periods may change. As the Bot develops, automated cleanup and configurable retention periods may be introduced or changed.
09
Deletion

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of 4n7 Moderation.

Depending on the request and the applicable legal requirements, we may need to verify the request before taking action.

Some information may need to be retained when required by law, necessary to establish or defend legal claims, needed for security, or otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

If the Bot has been removed from a server and its information is no longer required for a permitted feature or legal purpose, the corresponding data should be deleted or de-identified within a reasonable period.

10
Requests

Privacy & Data Requests

To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with 4n7 Moderation, contact us using the information provided in the Contact section of this policy.

To help locate the correct records, a request may need to include relevant Discord identifiers, such as a user ID or server ID, along with enough information to verify the request.

Do not send your Discord password, bot token, authentication token, or other account credentials as part of a privacy request.

!
Never send credentials. 4n7 Moderation will not require your Discord password or authentication token to process a normal privacy request.
